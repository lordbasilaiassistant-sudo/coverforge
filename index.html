Questions

Is CoverForge really free?

Yes. The entire cover designer runs 100% in your browser — pick a style, palette, format and font pairing, then export a high-resolution PNG with no signup, no server and no watermark. The optional Pro AI copywriter uses your own GLM key.

Do you use AI image generation?

No, and that's the point. Covers are built from generative typographic and geometric art — gradients, geometry and real Google Font pairings drawn on an HTML canvas. You get crisp, scalable, slop-free results you fully control.

What can I make covers for?

Book covers (2:3 portrait), podcast and album artwork (1:1 square), and wide social/banner art (16:9). Switch formats and the layout re-composes to fit.

How does the Pro AI copywriter work?

Paste your own GLM (z.ai) API key. CoverForge calls the GLM API directly from your browser to suggest title, subtitle and tagline options based on your topic and vibe. The key is stored only on this page and is sent only to the official GLM endpoint — never to us, because we have no server.

Who owns the covers I make?

You do. Everything is rendered locally from your inputs. There's no upload, no account and no watermark — download and use the PNG however you like.
